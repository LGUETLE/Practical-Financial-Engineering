# Lower and upper bounds (Andersen-Broadie duality)

Glasserman benchmark, basis degree 2 scaled by K. Lower bound L: forward pass of the policy on 200,000 independent paths. Upper bound U = L + gap, gap estimated with 4,000 outer paths and 2,000 inner paths per continuation value (inner simulations only at in-the-money dates). 95% interval [L - 1.96 SE_L, U + 1.96 SE_U]. All policies at one S0 use the same lower-bound, outer and inner random numbers (seed 2026). Generated by `bounds_engine.py`.
