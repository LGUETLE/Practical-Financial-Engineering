# Classic LSM vs TVR (S0 = 100, 50 replications)

Each replication: new training and pricing paths (N = 4,000 each), both methods on the same paths.
Global seed 2026. Generated by `theory_engine.py`.

| Method | Mean price | Variance | 95% CI lower | 95% CI upper |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Classic LSM | 13.7766 | 0.0555 | 13.7097 | 13.8436 |
| TVR Value Iteration | 13.8060 | 0.0505 | 13.7422 | 13.8699 |

Test for equal variances (H0: Var(LSM) = Var(TVR)):

| Test | Statistic | p-value |
|---|---:|---:|
| F-test, two-sided, df = (49, 49) | F = 1.0991 | 0.7421 |
| Pitman-Morgan (paired samples) | r = 0.1879 | 0.1914 |

The LSM and TVR prices are strongly correlated across replications (correlation 0.9690) because
both are computed on the same paths; the F-test assumes independent samples, the Pitman-Morgan
test accounts for the pairing. Neither rejects equal variances at the 5% level.
