# European max-call as control variate (Glasserman benchmark, TVR policy)

R = 50 replications per S0 (same paths as the 2x2 design, seed 2026), N = 4,000 training and 4,000 pricing paths. Control: discounted European payoff with known mean (Stulz closed form, dividend yield 0.1); coefficient b estimated on the training paths. 'VRF per run' = Var(Y) / Var(Y - bX) on the pricing paths, averaged over runs (pricing noise only). 'Var ratio across runs' = variance of the R plain prices / variance of the R CV prices (includes the training noise of the policy, which the control variate cannot remove). Generated by `theory_engine.py`.
